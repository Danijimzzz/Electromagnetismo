The Faraday cage

If a storm catches you in the countryside, the best place to wait is inside the car: even if lightning hits it, nothing happens to you. And in the elevator, on the other hand, the phone loses its signal. What do they have in common?

A little history

  1. 1755Benjamin Franklin hangs a cork ball inside a charged metal can and sees that it is not attracted: all the charge stays on the outside.
  2. 1836Michael Faraday lines a room with metal, charges it until sparks fly on the outside and goes in with an electroscope: he detects no charge.
  3. 1867James Clerk Maxwell uses the same reasoning to verify with great precision that the electric force goes as 1/r², like gravity.
  4. 1963After several accidents, aviation regulations require aircraft to conduct the current of a lightning strike along the outside and their tanks to withstand sparks.

Which tool it relates to

Electromagnetic waves · Electromagnetism

A wave entering a good conductor is attenuated exponentially. The distance at which its amplitude drops to 1/e is the penetration depth, and it depends on the frequency and the conductivity.

δ = 1/√(π f μ σ) , E(x) = E_0 e^{−x/δ}

It opens as a lossy medium: aluminium (σ = 3.5·107 S/m) and the phone signal at 900 MHz, looking at the field at 10 µm. We get δ = 2.84 µm and almost 3 % of the amplitude.
