This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use this website, under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).

What the tools do with your data

Nothing leaves your device. There are no accounts, forms or own server: the data you type, your saved exercises and your preferences (such as dark mode) are stored only in your browser's local storage, and the files you export (.simu.json and .emsim.json) are downloaded to your computer. The owner cannot see any of that. You can delete it whenever you want from your browser settings.

Visit counter

To know which tools are used the most (and to mark with a flame 🔥 the most used one in each subject), the website adds one visit each time you open a tool, at most once per session. Only the month, the tool and the number of visits are stored, in a counter of its own hosted on Cloudflare: your IP address is not stored, there are no cookies or identifiers, and what you do inside each tool or what you type into it is not measured. You are not counted if your browser sends the “Do Not Track” signal.

Hosting

The website is served from Cloudflare and Vercel. Like any web server, they receive your IP address and technical connection data (browser, page requested, date and time) in order to deliver the page, protect it from attacks and produce aggregate statistics. Legal basis: the legitimate interest in the website working and being secure (Art. 6.1.f GDPR). These companies may process the data outside the European Economic Area with the safeguards of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Advertising

The advertisements on this website are from Google AdSense (Google Ireland Limited), and Google may use cookies and identifiers to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud and, only if you accept, to personalize them according to your interests. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you give or refuse in the cookie notice and can withdraw at any time. If you do not give it, the ads are not personalized. More information in how Google uses data from sites that use its services. See also the cookie policy.

Emails you send me

If you write to the contact email, I use your address and your message only to reply to you, and I delete them when they are no longer needed. Legal basis: your own request (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability of your data by writing to the contact email. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Minors

The website does not ask anyone for data. If you are under 14, ask your parents or guardians to go over the cookie notice with you.
