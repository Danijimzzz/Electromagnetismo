Phase 10, finished: more tools with more resources

No new tools, but Mathematics, Statistics, Economics and General physics learn new things, and the tools talk to each other more.

🛠️In all the tools

  • More “Use in…” buttons: they take a result to another tool with the data already filled in (from Series to Taylor, from Laplace to Control, from Interest to Savings and Annuities, from Gases to Thermodynamic cycles and from Projectile motion to Work and energy, among others).
  • Edge cases in Mathematics: more than 7,000 awkward inputs have been tried (empty, zeros, huge, with unclosed brackets…) and the tools give a clear message instead of an odd result or a frozen page.

📐Mathematics

  • Taylor: radius and interval of convergence of any function, limits at infinity and a button to draw the function with its polynomial in the Grapher.
  • Series: integral test with antiderivative, sum of those that converge very slowly with a bounded remainder and a button to see the series as a Taylor expansion.
  • Derivatives: more integrals and limits solved and checked; numerical limits are cross-checked near the point.
  • Matrices and Complex numbers: kernel, image and subspaces, non-rational eigenvectors and cells that accept calculations.
  • Surfaces: points without a derivative (peaks and edges) and critical points outside the drawn range.
  • Laplace: stability of F(s) from its poles and a button to use it as an open loop in Control. ODE: button to solve with Laplace.

📊Statistics

  • Distributions: central limit theorem, exact versus approximate and two curves at once.
  • Intervals: four methods for the proportion, prediction and tolerance.
  • Regression and Multiple regression: Fisher's z, comparison of correlations, weighted fit, nearly collinear data and a VIF warning.
  • Probability, Descriptive and Errors: discrete random variable, 2 × 2 table, geometric, harmonic and weighted means, and propagation with correlated variables.
  • “Use in…” buttons between Descriptive, Intervals, Tests, ANOVA and Non-parametric.

💶Economics

  • Own practice mode in 21 tools: random exercises for each one, with their statement and the correction of what you type.
  • Visible convention: the working of Interest, Loans, Annuities, Savings, Bonds, NPV and IRR, Depreciation, Black-Scholes and Exchange rates now ends by saying which day-count basis, which rate per period and which rounding it uses.

  • Regression on economic data: residual plot and a button to add more variables in Multiple regression.
  • Game theory: 3 × 3 matrices with elimination of dominated strategies round by round.
  • More “Use in…”: from Interest to Savings and Annuities, from Multiplier to IS-LM and from GDP to Index numbers.

🔭General physics

  • Units: the constants move to CODATA 2022, with the 2018 edition as an option.
  • Waves: the speed of sound can be given from the air temperature.
  • “Use in…” buttons: from Projectile motion to Work and energy, from Gases to Thermodynamic cycles (when it is a Carnot cycle) and from Gravitation to Modern physics.
