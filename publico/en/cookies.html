Cookie policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser. This website also uses the browser's local storage, which works in a similar way. Here is what is stored and what for.

Own storage (necessary)

The tools store in your browser only what is essential to work. It is not sent anywhere or used to identify you, so it does not need your consent (Art. 22.2 LSSI).

NamePurposeDuration
<tool>-v1 (for example ondas-v1)The data you had in each tool, so that it is still there when you come backUntil you delete it
simu-ejercicios:<tool>Your exercises saved in My exercisesUntil you delete it
simu-ejercicios-uiWhether the My exercises sidebar is open and which folders you have closedUntil you delete it
simu-temaLight or dark mode, if you have chosen it with the buttonUntil you delete it
electrostatica-v1, electro-sim-ui-v2Electrostatics: your problems, the one you had open and the view preferencesUntil you delete it
simu-ejercicios-activo:<tool>, simu-practica:<tool>The open exercise and the data from before you entered practice modeUntil you close the tab

Advertising cookies (third-party, with your consent)

For now the website shows no ads and installs none of these cookies. This section will apply as soon as they are enabled.

Google AdSense uses cookies, such as __gads, __gpi, IDE or NID, to show ads, limit how many times you see the same one, measure their performance and, if you accept, personalize them. They last up to 13 months. Google manages them: you can see the full list in the cookies used by Google advertising.

These cookies are only installed if you accept them in the cookie notice that appears the first time you visit. If you reject them, Google may show non-personalized ads, which use only what is needed to limit frequency and prevent fraud.

How to change your decision

  • When there are ads, use the Cookie preferences link at the bottom of any tool to see the notice again.
  • You can also delete or block cookies and local storage from your browser settings. If you delete local storage, you will lose the exercises you have not exported.
  • For Google personalization in general: Google Ad Settings.
